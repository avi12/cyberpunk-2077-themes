Night City after dark, in your browser.

A dark HUD theme for your browser: near-black frame, cyan tab text, red alerts, and the neon skyline behind every new tab. No scripts, no permissions, no data collected.

For the full browser Cyberpunk overhaul, use Edge+ (https://github.com/avi12/edge-plus) with Cyberpunk 2077 New Tab (https://github.com/avi12/cyberpunk-2077-new-tab)
